Whoop Dee Doo / Portland

TBA installation

A room for the brain.

An interactive installation for the Time-Based Art Festival, presented through Whoop Dee Doo.

Part of a larger Whoop Dee Doo environment themed around the body, this room imagined the brain as a space visitors could enter. A sculptural brain could be manipulated to control projected “memories.” Arduino, tilt switches, Max/MSP, and found video connected the object to the changing image. Discarded materials—including carpet, trash bags, and metallic bowls—formed the surrounding room.
