Inside UnfoldMade for exploration

Less planning.
More listening.

01

Begin with something small.

Generate a phrase, choose a key and scale, and adjust the notes by ear. Add related follower tracks, each with its own pattern.

02

Give each voice its own sound.

Use the Mac’s built-in piano to get started, or load your installed AU and VST3 instruments and effects. Layer up to eight tracks.

03

Let it change. Keep what matters.

Explore Still, Drift, and Renew behavior. Save editable Pieces and Keep snapshots, then record a stereo take and export a WAV.
